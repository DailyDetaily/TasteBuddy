##### High-End Traditional Korean Restaurant, La Yeon

Korean restaurant La Yeon offers the traditional tastes of Korea in an elaborate and delicate fashion. Korean haute cuisine prepared with care is served with elegance and style.

![Image 22: Royal Casserole / Platter of Nine Delicacies](http://www.shilla.net/images/contents/dining/KRN/R0000001TZPL_EN.jpg)

![Image 23: La Yeon  Introduction (see details at the bottom)](http://www.shilla.net/images/contents/dining/KRN/R0000001QL1W_EN.jpg)

Using the highest quality local and seasonal ingredients, La Yeon interprets traditional cooking methods into contemporary taste.
